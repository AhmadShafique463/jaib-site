How it works

Your alerts become your record

Every payment already sends you an alert, by notification, text or email. JAIB reads them where they arrive, on your phone, and keeps the payments.

Private by design

Your money picture never leaves your phone

JAIB was built so that we never hold your money data. There is nothing on our side to leak, sell or lose.

  • Your record is kept on your phone, encrypted, behind your PIN or fingerprint.
  • No JAIB account: nothing about your money is stored on a JAIB server.
  • No permission to read your SMS: JAIB reads only the payment alerts your phone shows.
  • Chats, one-time passwords and promotions are read in memory and dropped at once.
  • Your money data is never sold and never given to advertisers.
  • Delete everything from the app at any time.

How JAIB uses Gmail

When you first open JAIB you sign in with Google, and JAIB asks for read-only access to your Gmail. Many banks send payment alerts only by email, and this is how JAIB reads them.

  • JAIB searches only for emails from bank, wallet, utility and shop senders on its list, and from senders you add. It does not read the rest of your mail.
  • The emails are read on your phone and turned into transactions there. They leave the phone only if you choose to send the JAIB team a report from your own email app, and then only as templates with every name, amount, number and address removed, which you see first.
  • JAIB never sends, deletes or changes your email.

JAIB's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Disconnect Gmail at any time in JAIB's Sources, or remove JAIB's access in your Google Account.
