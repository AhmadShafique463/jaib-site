What's new in JAIB

Every JAIB release since work began, newest first. The blog explains the bigger ones. Each update reaches testers in the closed beta first, and this page grows with it. Have an idea? Write to hello@jaib.pk.

  1. 17 November 2026

    jaib.pk opens

    • JAIB has its own website and email, hello@jaib.pk, with a person behind it who replies within 20 hours.
  2. 16 November 2026 · 0.2.40

    More room, tidier pages

    • More room on your lists. On Budgets, Coming up, Savings goals, Categories and Rules, the big red button is now a small + low on the right, so your list gets the screen.
    • Tidier pages. Activity shows Month so far once, an account opens on its payments, and buttons look like buttons.
    • The category picker holds still. The Change category sheet opens and stays put, and its list scrolls.
    • Read the post
  3. 16 November 2026 · 0.2.39

    Colour for accounts and categories

    • A colour for each account, shown on its badge everywhere, so you tell your accounts apart at a glance.
    • Categories in colour in + Add and on payments, so you find Groceries or Fuel without reading.
    • A budget opens like every page: it slides in, and Back slides it out. The budget widget shows only what fits when you make it smaller.
    • Read the post
  4. 15 November 2026 · 0.2.38

    Nothing missed

    • Alerts that came while JAIB was stopped are read when it starts again. They go to Review for you to check, never straight into your totals.
    • If JAIB ever closes on its own, Home says so next time and that nothing was lost. Diagnostics are sent only if you choose to.
    • Read the post
  5. 15 November 2026 · 0.2.37

    An app you can feel

    • Buttons and switches you can feel: rows press in, switches tick, and a wrong PIN is felt as well as seen.
    • Pages that arrive in order, one row after another, so you see what changed.
    • No more blank pages: Budgets, Savings goals, Coming up and Insights show their shape while your figures load.
    • Read the post
  6. 15 November 2026 · 0.2.36

    Pull to refresh

    • Pull down to refresh, with the JAIB coin, on Home, Activity and Accounts: JAIB checks your alerts, your Gmail and the exchange rates.
    • Budget names in full, on their own line.
    • Easier to read at a large font on People, Coming up and Home.
    • Read the post
  7. 10 November 2026 · 0.2.35

    Budgets everywhere

    • Your budgets on the home screen: a widget with Safe to spend a day and the budgets that need you most.
    • Every Insights card explains itself: how its figure is worked out and the payments behind it.
    • A page for each budget, and budgets on Home if you want them there.
    • Loan reminders, and what a loan costs: 3 days before a loan is due and on the day, both ways.
    • Read the post
  8. 8 to 9 November 2026 · 0.2.32 to 0.2.34

    Help, one tap away

    • Your bank's help: open a bank or wallet in Accounts to call its helpline, block a card or report fraud, with numbers and steps from the bank's own website. JAIB also reads more banks' alert texts.
    • The Assistant knows budgets, goals, bills and people, and Take me there opens the page.
    • Guides on sample money the first time you open a page, so your own money is never shown or changed.
    • What's new, all of it: after an update, everything you missed, even across several versions.
    • Groundwork for a new Assistant model.
  9. 6 November 2026 · 0.2.28 to 0.2.31

    A menu and Kharcha

    • A menu at the top of each tab for Display, Security, Sources, backups and Help.
    • Kharcha, a tab of its own for budgets, bills, goals, people and Insights.
    • Newer encryption and security libraries, and a smarter check for possible duplicates.
  10. 5 November 2026 · 0.2.24 to 0.2.27

    Plan your money

    • Budgets and Safe to spend: monthly limits per category, and what you can spend each day.
    • Home-screen widgets: net worth, + Add, the Assistant and recent payments.
    • Rules with when and then: a shop or person, words in the message, an account or an amount.
    • Savings goals, bills and subscriptions, and Insights: where your money went and your net worth over time.
    • People: who owes you, whom you owe, and settling up in part or in full.
    • What JAIB did: everything JAIB did by itself, with Undo, behind the bell on Home.
  11. 3 to 5 November 2026 · 0.2.19 to 0.2.23

    Categories and a clearer Activity

    • A categories page, and 17 new categories.
    • Activity one month at a time, with money in, money out and a total for each day.
    • Paid to and Received from in + Add, and refunds you add by hand lower your spending.
    • More bank, wallet, biller and broker email senders read in Gmail.
  12. 30 October to 2 November 2026 · 0.2.13 to 0.2.18

    Figures you can trust

    • A Sources page, a balance self-check, and transfers between your own accounts matched in pairs.
    • Whole-number money, a running balance, and approving the payments that look right in one go.
    • Refunds by category, How is this worked out, an evening reminder, Edit Home, and net worth by currency.
    • A fix for a crash at launch on Android 11.
  13. 29 October 2026 · 0.2.12

    The closed beta begins

    • The first release to testers on Google Play's closed test.
    • Empty pages that teach you what goes there.
  14. 25 to 28 October 2026 · 0.2.5 to 0.2.11

    Learning JAIB

    • A first-run tour, first-time hints, and a How do I… page that works without the Assistant.
    • The Assistant explains how to do things, and a New rule page.
    • Bank texts that arrive in Truecaller or your SMS app are read from their notifications, once you agree.
    • Bank emails that land in Gmail's spam are read too, from many more bank and wallet senders.
    • A battery step so alerts keep coming, and an in-app update prompt.
  15. 22 to 23 October 2026 · 0.2.0 to 0.2.4

    The first full build

    • The first full-feature build for internal testing: payment alerts read as they arrive, Gmail, and the Assistant on your phone.
    • Receipt totals read correctly, and back-to-back alerts and duplicates across apps handled.
  16. 12 to 17 October 2026

    Work begins

    • The project starts with security defaults and automatic checks on every change.
    • The first internal release, with Google sign-in.
