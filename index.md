# JAIB

**Your money, on your phone.** JAIB is a personal money app for Android, made in Pakistan. It builds your spending
record from the payment alerts your banks and wallets already send you, so you do not have to type in every payment.

JAIB is in a closed beta on Android.

## What it does

- **Reads your payment alerts.** When a bank or wallet sends you an alert, by notification, text or email, JAIB turns
  it into a transaction: the amount, the account and where the money went.
- **Shows where your money goes.** Activity by day and month, categories, budgets, bills coming up, savings goals and
  who owes whom.
- **Stays on your phone.** Your money record is kept on your phone, encrypted, behind your PIN or fingerprint. There
  is no JAIB account and no JAIB server holding your money data.

## How JAIB uses Gmail

When you first open JAIB you sign in with Google, and JAIB asks Google for read-only access to your Gmail. Many banks
send payment alerts only by email, and this is how JAIB reads them.

- JAIB searches only for emails from bank, wallet, utility and shop senders on its list, and from any sender you add.
  It does not read the rest of your mail.
- The emails are read on your phone and turned into transactions there. They leave the phone only if you choose to
  send the JAIB team a report from your own email app, and then only as templates with every name, amount, number and
  address removed, which you see first.
- JAIB never sends, deletes or changes your email.
- JAIB's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
  [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
  including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Gmail at any time in JAIB's Sources, or remove JAIB's access from your
[Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
