# JAIB terms of service

Last updated: 18 November 2026 (beta).

These terms are the agreement between you and JAIB, the app and its makers ("JAIB", "we"), for using the JAIB app for
Android and this website. By installing or using JAIB you agree to them. If you do not agree, do not use JAIB. How JAIB
handles your data is in the [privacy policy](/privacy/), which is part of these terms.

## In short

- JAIB keeps a record of your spending from the payment alerts your banks and wallets send you. It is a record keeper,
  not a bank: it never moves money and never logs in to your bank.
- Your record stays on your phone, encrypted. There is no JAIB account, so keeping your phone, passphrase and backups
  safe is up to you.
- JAIB reads alerts automatically and can get one wrong. Check anything important against your bank.
- JAIB is not financial advice.

## Who can use JAIB

You must be at least 18, or have a parent's or guardian's permission to use JAIB. You must have the right to use the
phone, the Google account and the email addresses you connect to JAIB. Use JAIB only for your own money, or money you
are allowed to keep a record of.

## What JAIB does and does not do

- JAIB reads payment alerts from your notifications, the bank texts your messages app shows, and the emails from banks,
  wallets and shops in the Gmail accounts you connect (read-only). From them it makes a record of what you spent and
  received, with budgets, goals, bills and reports.
- JAIB does not hold, send, receive or move money, and is not connected to or endorsed by any bank or wallet. Names of
  banks and wallets belong to their owners.
- JAIB is not a bank, an accountant, a tax adviser or a financial adviser. What it shows, including the JAIB Assistant's
  answers, budgets and insights, is for your own information. Decide for yourself, or ask a qualified person, before
  making a money decision.

## Accuracy

JAIB reads alerts by matching their wording, and banks change their wording. It can miss a payment, read an amount or
date wrong, put a payment in the wrong category or count one twice. Balances JAIB shows are estimates from the alerts it
has seen, not your bank's figures. Your bank's own statement is the true record. You can correct anything in the app,
and you can send us a "wrong result" report so JAIB learns.

## Your phone, your data, your backups

- There is no JAIB account and no copy of your record on our servers. If you lose your phone, uninstall JAIB or delete
  its data without a backup, your record is gone, and we cannot bring it back.
- Backups are encrypted with your passphrase. We cannot open them or reset the passphrase. Keep your passphrase and
  recovery key safe.
- Anyone who can unlock your phone and JAIB can see your record. Use JAIB's lock, and remember that a home-screen widget
  shows figures outside the lock unless you hide amounts.
- You can delete everything at any time: Menu > About > Delete all my data. The privacy policy explains each step.

## Google sign-in and Gmail

JAIB asks you to sign in with Google so it can read your bank and wallet emails on your phone. Your Google account is
covered by Google's own terms. You can disconnect Gmail in JAIB's Sources, or remove JAIB's access in your Google account
at any time; JAIB then stops reading your email.

  you cancel; cancel in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan stays until

- If we change a price, Google Play tells you before it applies, and you can cancel.
