JAIB now has its own home on the web, at jaib.pk.

JAIB is a money app for Pakistan. It builds your spending record from the payment alerts your banks and wallets already
send, and keeps that record on your phone, encrypted. There is no JAIB account and nothing about your money on our servers.

What you will find here:

- [How JAIB works](/#how), and the [banks and wallets](/#works) whose alerts it reads.
- [Every update](/updates/) since work began on 12 October 2026, and posts like this one about what each update brings.
- Our [privacy policy](/privacy/), in plain words.

You can also write to us at [hello@jaib.pk](mailto:hello@jaib.pk). A person replies within 20 hours. Problems get fixed,
and good ideas go into the app.

JAIB is in a closed beta on Android. Follow along on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/people/JAIB/61596306664204/),
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/145438013/), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@jaibapp) and
[TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@jaibpk).
