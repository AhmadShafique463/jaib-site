JAIB plans

Free during the beta. Everyone in the closed beta is a Bunyaadi user, on Bunyaad: everything in JAIB, with nothing to pay. Plans go on sale after the beta. Become a Bunyaadi user.

Reading your payment alerts and getting the numbers right is free on every plan, always. Paid plans add room and extras.

Khazana

How paying works

  • Google Play takes the payment, and shows the price before you pay. JAIB never sees your card or wallet.
  • Plans renew every month until you cancel in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. When you cancel, you keep the plan until the month you paid for ends.
  • Moving down a plan never deletes anything. Accounts, rules and categories you already have keep working.
  • New phone, or JAIB reinstalled? Open Menu > About > Your plan > Restore purchases, with the same Google account you paid with.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. You can also write to hello@jaib.pk.

Prices are in Pakistani rupees and may change; Google Play tells you before a new price applies. The full rules are in the terms of service and the privacy policy.
